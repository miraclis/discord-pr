SOLONG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Solong, a browser-based game and Discord Activity.

The current version of Solong does not require an account, does not operate its own user database, and does not intentionally collect or store personal Discord profile information.

1. Information Solong Collects

In its current version, Solong does not intentionally collect personal information directly from users.

Solong currently does not provide:

  • user registration or Solong accounts;
  • a persistent user profile;
  • wallet connection;
  • payment processing;
  • advertising;
  • behavioral advertising or analytics tracking;
  • a server-side leaderboard or persistent gameplay database.

2. Discord

Solong can run as a Discord Activity. Discord is a separate service and processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

The current version of Solong initializes the Discord Embedded App environment but does not intentionally request, store, or maintain Discord account profile data in a Solong database.

If future versions introduce Discord login, user profiles, achievements, leaderboards, or other account-linked features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are made publicly available.

3. Hosting and Technical Information

Solong is hosted using third-party infrastructure provided by Cloudflare.

Like most web hosting and security providers, Cloudflare may automatically process technical information associated with network requests, such as IP address, request metadata, browser or device information, and security-related data.

This processing is performed by Cloudflare as part of providing hosting, security, reliability, and network services and is subject to Cloudflare's own privacy terms and practices.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The current version of Solong does not intentionally use cookies or similar technologies for advertising or behavioral analytics.

Third-party platforms used to deliver Solong, including Discord and Cloudflare, may use their own necessary technologies in accordance with their respective policies.

5. Game Data

Current gameplay information such as score, elapsed time, discovered tiles, hints, and board state is used to operate the current game session.

The current public version of Solong does not intentionally send this gameplay state to a Solong-operated backend database for persistent storage.

6. Sharing of Information

Because Solong does not currently maintain its own database of personal user information, it does not sell personal information or sell user profiles to advertisers.

Technical information may still be processed by infrastructure providers such as Discord and Cloudflare as described above.

7. Data Retention

The current version of Solong does not intentionally maintain persistent server-side personal user records.

Third-party service providers may retain technical or account-related information according to their own retention policies.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to keep Solong and its deployment infrastructure secure.

However, no online service or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Solong is not intended to override or replace the minimum age requirements that apply to Discord or other platforms through which Solong is accessed.

Users must meet the applicable minimum age requirements for the services they use to access Solong.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing.

Because the current version of Solong does not intentionally maintain its own persistent personal user database, there may be no Solong-held personal record to access or delete.

Requests concerning information processed directly by Discord or Cloudflare should generally be directed to those providers.

11. International Services

Solong relies on online infrastructure that may process technical information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Such processing by third-party providers is governed by their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Solong adds or changes features, especially if future versions introduce accounts, authentication, a backend database, leaderboards, wallet connections, analytics, or payments.

The latest revision date will always be shown at the top of this page.
